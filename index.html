Who We Are

Pioneering Student-Led Scientific Discovery

The Student Research Council (SRC) is a premier student-driven research and innovation council. We bridge the gap between academic theories and tangible breakthroughs, providing students across all disciplines with the platform, resources, and mentorship to push frontiers.

At SRC, students are empowered to collaborate, experiment fearlessly, and convert intellectual curiosity into real-world solutions that impact society:

  • ✓ Explore cutting-edge research in emerging technology paradigms.
  • ✓ Develop innovative ideas with structured faculty mentorship.
  • ✓ Participate in national & global competitions, hackathons, and symposia.
  • ✓ Attend technical workshops, paper reading groups, and lab bootcamps.
  • ✓ Collaborate across disciplines with peer researchers and developers.
  • ✓ Convert theoretical ideas into practical solutions and patentable prototypes.
  • ✓ Develop vital problem-solving & publication skills for academia and industry.

Council Domains

From Ideas to Impact

We provide an end-to-end framework that accelerates student concepts from raw curiosity into verified scientific models and scalable solutions.

Research

Encouraging students to explore deep-tech questions, scientific paradigms, and emerging technologies through systematic methodology and faculty guidance.

Innovation

Turning creative sparks and abstract ideas into meaningful solutions and functional MVPs designed to resolve critical real-world challenges.

Collaboration

Connecting students, dedicated faculty advisors, industry researchers, and cross-department innovators into high-synergy research pods.

Competitions

Providing competitive platforms, ideathons, and national hackathons where students benchmark, defend, and showcase their inventions.

Workshops

Hands-on learning through intensive technical bootcamps, scientific LaTeX drafting masterclasses, and experimental laboratory sessions.

Entrepreneurship

Encouraging student investigators to transform laboratory breakthroughs and intellectual property into viable, venture-backed startup opportunities.
