About SRC

Discover the vision, mission, core values, and historical milestones driving the Student Research Council to cultivate future scientists and innovators.

Origin & Purpose

Our Story

The Student Research Council (SRC) was founded with a singular, resolute conviction: that undergraduate and postgraduate students possess the limitless curiosity required to solve some of the world's most difficult technological and scientific problems.

Traditionally, high-tier research opportunities were siloed within senior research labs or inaccessible to early-year students. SRC emerged to dismantle these barriers. We created a collaborative, cross-departmental hub where students could access faculty mentorship, experimental laboratory equipment, computation resources, and peer review.

Today, SRC stands as an officially recognized student research body, fostering an environment where ideas are rigorously nurtured from conceptual hypotheses into published academic papers, working hardware prototypes, and scalable technological innovations.

🏛️ The SRC Charter

"To democratize inquiry across every discipline, connect ideas across academic boundaries, and empower students to transform knowledge into meaningful real-world impact."

KEY FOCUS AREAS:
SRC is interdisciplinary by design — supporting research, innovation, and real-world problem solving across all academic domains, from Engineering, Technology, AI, and Computer Science to Natural Sciences, Life Sciences, Health, Environment, Social Sciences, Humanities, Business, Design, and emerging interdisciplinary fields.
No discipline stands alone. SRC welcomes domain-specific research as well as projects that combine perspectives, methods, and expertise from multiple fields.
👁️

Our Vision

To establish an internationally recognized, student-driven center of research excellence that nurtures bold scientific minds, pushes technological frontiers, and empowers every student to translate curiosity into lasting global impact.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive mentorship, laboratory resources, seed research funding, and competitive platforms that enable students to build innovative solutions, publish peer-reviewed papers, and launch technology-driven ventures.

Ethos & Culture

Our Core Values

Scientific Pipeline

How SRC Guides Research

A structured, 4-stage pipeline that takes a student from an initial spark of an idea to an evaluated and published project.

01

Ideation & Inquiry

Brainstorming circles under Curiominds and formulating specific, testable research hypotheses.

02

Mentorship & Design

Pairing with faculty advisors, designing rigorous experiment methodology, and literature synthesis.

03

Prototyping & Testing

Accessing university lab benches, cloud compute credits, and conducting controlled experiments.

04

Dissemination & Launch

Authoring academic papers, presenting at conferences, patent filing, or incubator venture launch.
